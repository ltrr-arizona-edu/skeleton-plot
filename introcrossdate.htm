Introduction

Welcome to the web presentation on crossdating tree rings by skeleton plotting:

  • Produced at the Laboratory of Tree-Ring Research, The University of Arizona, Tucson
    • Author: Research Specialist Senior Paul R. Sheppard
    • Principal Investigators: Director Malcolm K. Hughes and Associate Professor Thomas W. Swetnam
  • Objective: To inform about dendrochronological crossdating using skeleton plots
  • Note: Other ways exist to crossdate; visit the Laboratory of Tree-Ring Research for more information
Important Point: This presentation has interactive figures:
  • They work in any up-to-date web browser, with a mouse or on a touch screen
  • You will be able to interact with this presentation, including trying skeleton plotting for yourself!
